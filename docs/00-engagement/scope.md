# Central Therapy Digital Platform — Project Scope

**Project:** Central Therapy Digital Platform  
**Phase:** Phase 0 — Engagement & Project Foundation  
**Document Status:** Draft  
**Owner:** Technical Lead  
**Client:** Central Therapy  

---

## 1. Purpose

This document defines the initial boundaries of the Central Therapy Digital
Platform project.

The purpose of the scope is to establish what the project is expected to
deliver, identify work that is outside the current engagement, and provide a
baseline for managing future changes.

The scope may be refined following discovery where new technical, operational
or business requirements are identified.

---

## 2. Project Scope

The project covers the modernisation of Central Therapy's public digital
presence and the establishment of supporting technology for website operation,
search visibility, analytics, marketing and Microsoft 365 integration.

The project consists of the following primary workstreams.

---

## 3. Website Rebuild

### In Scope

The existing Central Therapy website will be replaced with a modern,
responsive website.

The work includes:

- new website architecture;
- responsive mobile-first design;
- desktop, laptop, tablet and mobile layouts;
- navigation redesign;
- homepage redesign;
- service pages;
- funding and eligibility information;
- practitioner and business information;
- contact functionality;
- enquiry functionality;
- referral pathways where required;
- appointment booking integration;
- reusable website components;
- content management capability;
- accessibility considerations;
- performance optimisation;
- browser and device compatibility;
- production deployment.

The existing website will remain available where practical until the
replacement website is ready for production.

---

## 4. User Experience and Design

### In Scope

The project includes:

- information architecture;
- sitemap development;
- user journey mapping;
- wireframes;
- responsive layouts;
- design system;
- typography;
- spacing;
- reusable UI components;
- calls to action;
- form experience;
- navigation behaviour;
- mobile interaction design;
- conversion-focused page design.

The design should provide a consistent Central Therapy brand experience across
supported devices.

---

## 5. Search Engine Optimisation

### In Scope

SEO work includes:

- current SEO baseline assessment;
- existing URL inventory;
- indexation assessment;
- keyword research;
- search-intent analysis;
- page architecture;
- on-page SEO;
- technical SEO;
- metadata;
- heading structure;
- internal linking;
- XML sitemap;
- robots configuration;
- canonical configuration;
- structured data where appropriate;
- image optimisation;
- local SEO considerations;
- redirect mapping;
- migration SEO validation;
- Search Console configuration or integration where appropriate;
- ongoing SEO measurement.

Existing search equity should be protected during migration where reasonably
possible.

---

## 6. Content

### In Scope

Content work may include:

- auditing existing website content;
- reorganising existing content;
- rewriting or improving website copy;
- identifying content gaps;
- service-page content structure;
- funding-page content structure;
- FAQ content;
- landing-page content;
- SEO-focused content planning;
- resource/article architecture;
- calls to action;
- content migration.

Clinical or professional claims requiring specialist verification remain
subject to client approval.

---

## 7. Analytics and Conversion Measurement

### In Scope

The project includes establishing measurement for important website activity.

Potential measurements include:

- website traffic;
- traffic sources;
- landing-page performance;
- appointment booking interactions;
- enquiry submissions;
- referral interactions;
- phone-link interactions;
- email-link interactions;
- conversion rates;
- marketing campaign performance;
- relevant user behaviour.

Analytics requirements and platforms will be confirmed during discovery and
solution design.

---

## 8. Microsoft 365 Integration

### In Scope

The website may integrate with Central Therapy's Microsoft 365 environment
where there is a documented business requirement.

Potential integration areas include:

- Exchange Online / Outlook;
- Microsoft Teams;
- Microsoft Graph;
- SharePoint;
- OneDrive;
- Power Automate;
- Microsoft Bookings where appropriate.

Specific integrations will be selected following assessment of Central
Therapy's workflows and Microsoft 365 environment.

Microsoft 365 migration activities that are separate from the website will be
tracked independently where required.

---

## 9. Digital Marketing Infrastructure

### In Scope

The project will establish website capabilities required to support digital
marketing.

This includes:

- campaign landing pages;
- conversion tracking;
- campaign attribution;
- SEO content infrastructure;
- local search support;
- analytics integration;
- marketing measurement;
- calls to action;
- lead-generation pathways;
- ongoing optimisation capability.

Marketing campaigns themselves will be managed as an ongoing workstream rather
than considered complete when the website launches.

---

## 10. Security and Privacy

### In Scope

The project includes:

- secure development practices;
- environment-variable management;
- protection of credentials;
- HTTPS;
- form validation;
- input sanitisation;
- spam protection;
- dependency management;
- access-control considerations;
- logging and monitoring;
- backup and recovery planning;
- security configuration;
- privacy considerations for information collected through the website.

Special consideration will be given to workflows involving personal or
health-related information.

The public website should not become a clinical record system unless that
requirement is separately assessed and approved.

- conversion optimisation;

- backups;
- ongoing website maintenance.

Commercial arrangements for ongoing support and marketing will be defined

---

## 15. Out of Scope

Unless subsequently approved through scope change, the following are outside
the initial project scope:

- development of a complete clinical practice-management system;
- replacement of specialist healthcare software;
- electronic medical records;
- storage of clinical records within the public website;
- custom accounting software;
- custom payroll software;
- native iOS application;
- native Android application;
- unrelated internal IT infrastructure;
- hardware procurement;
- custom AI platform development;
- functionality unrelated to Central Therapy's agreed digital platform.

A future requirement may be added through the project change process rather
than assumed to be included.

---

## 19. Scope Change

A material request that changes project cost, timeline, architecture or
deliverables should be assessed before implementation.

Material scope changes should record:

1. requested change;
2. business reason;
3. technical impact;
4. timeline impact;
5. cost impact where applicable;
6. risks;
7. decision;
8. approval.

Approved changes will be recorded in the project change log.
