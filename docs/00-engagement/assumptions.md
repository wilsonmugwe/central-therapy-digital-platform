# Central Therapy Digital Platform — Assumptions

**Project:** Central Therapy Digital Platform  
**Phase:** Phase 0 — Engagement & Project Foundation  
**Document Status:** Active  
**Owner:** Technical Lead

---

## 1. Purpose

This document records assumptions being used for initial project planning.

Assumptions are not confirmed facts.

Each material assumption should be validated during discovery. Incorrect
assumptions may affect project scope, architecture, timeline, cost or risk.

---

## 2. Business Assumptions

### ASM-001

Central Therapy intends to replace the existing website rather than perform
only a cosmetic modification.

**Status:** Confirmed

### ASM-002

The existing Central Therapy domain will continue to be used.

**Status:** Assumed

### ASM-003

The website is expected to contribute to business growth through enquiries,
bookings, referrals and other appropriate conversion pathways.

**Status:** Confirmed

### ASM-004

Central Therapy intends to actively improve its SEO and digital marketing.

**Status:** Confirmed

### ASM-005

The client will provide reasonable access to systems required for the project.

**Status:** Assumed

## 10. Assumption Management

If an assumption proves incorrect:

1. document the finding;
2. identify affected requirements;
3. assess scope impact;
4. assess architecture impact;
5. assess timeline impact;
6. assess cost impact where applicable;
7. update the risk register;
8. raise a change request where necessary.
