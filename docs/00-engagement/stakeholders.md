# Central Therapy Digital Platform — Stakeholders

**Project:** Central Therapy Digital Platform  
**Phase:** Phase 0 — Engagement & Project Foundation  
**Document Status:** Draft  
**Owner:** Technical Lead  

---

## 1. Purpose

This document identifies the stakeholders involved in or affected by the
Central Therapy Digital Platform project.

It establishes initial responsibilities, interests, influence and approval
requirements.

The stakeholder register will be refined during discovery.

---

## 2. Primary Stakeholders

**Role:** Project Sponsor / Client Representative

**Interest:** High  
**Influence:** High

## 3. Website Users

### STK-003 — Prospective Clients

People researching Central Therapy's services.

Primary interests include:

- understanding available services;
- determining whether Central Therapy can help;
- understanding funding options;
- finding service-area information;
- contacting Central Therapy;
- booking an appointment.

---

### STK-004 — Existing Clients

Existing Central Therapy clients who may use the website to:

- locate contact information;
- access service information;
- access booking pathways;
- find useful resources.

---

### STK-005 — Family Members and Carers

People researching or coordinating services on behalf of another person.

Their experience should be considered when designing:

- service information;
- funding information;
- contact pathways;
- referral pathways;
- booking pathways.

---

### STK-006 — Referrers and Healthcare Professionals

Potential users may include:

- general practitioners;
- allied health professionals;
- support coordinators;
- plan managers;
- aged-care providers;
- other authorised referrers.

Their requirements will be assessed during discovery before a referral
workflow is designed.

## 5. Approval Responsibilities

The following approval model is proposed.

| Area | Technical Lead | Client |
|---|---|---|
| Technical architecture | Responsible | Informed |
| Business requirements | Facilitates | Approves |
| Website design | Recommends | Approves |
| Business content | Implements | Approves |
| Clinical/professional claims | Does not independently validate | Approves |
| SEO implementation | Responsible | Informed |
| Marketing direction | Recommends/implements as agreed | Approves |
| Security architecture | Responsible | Informed |
| Scope changes | Assesses | Approves |
| Production launch | Recommends readiness | Approves |

---

## 6. Stakeholder Management

Stakeholder requirements, concerns and approvals should be documented where
they materially affect:

- scope;
- requirements;
- architecture;
- design;
- content;
- privacy;
- marketing;
- timeline;
- budget;
- production readiness.
